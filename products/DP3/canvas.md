# DP3: Global Participation Insights: product canvas

> Synthetic project. All roles are fictional. The headings are the same for every product.

## Customer
Executives and the international growth lead.

## Problem
There is no comparable count of players across countries, and a single number hides how uncertain it is.

## Value proposition
**Driver for G1 (directly moves the goal).**

## Consumption type
Internal.

## Product type
Insights.

## Owner role and supporting roles
Data Product Manager, NFL FLAG Insights (fictional). Supporting: analytics, international growth.

## Key dependencies
DP2 (people), the counting rules (ADR-005), the scorecard and its methodology version, the uncertainty decomposition (ADR-015), federation returns.

## What we will NOT do
No single number without its range. No country ranking where a country has too few records to publish (suppression applies). No claim about countries that sent no data.

## Expected shelf life
Medium-term; reviewed each season and restated when the methodology changes (ADR-007).
