# DP4: US Flag Growth & Club Market Insights: product canvas

> Synthetic project. All roles are fictional. The headings are the same for every product.

## Customer
NFL FLAG domestic operations and club youth and community managers.

## Problem
Domestic league data is sparse and uneven. Growth cannot be told apart from better reporting, and a club cannot see how much of its market is missing.

## Value proposition
**Driver for G2 (directly moves the goal).**

## Consumption type
Internal, plus club-facing aggregates.

## Product type
Insights.

## Owner role and supporting roles
Data Product Manager, NFL FLAG Insights (fictional). Supporting: data steward, analytics, club partner.

## Key dependencies
DP1 (intake and the data standard), DP2 (people), the club market approximation (ADR-016), purpose-specific data quality grades (ADR-014), the club share.

## What we will NOT do
No club territory claim: markets are a project approximation. No cell under the small-cell size. No ranking of individual leagues for the public. No person-level field in any club share.

## Expected shelf life
Long-term; reviewed annually.
