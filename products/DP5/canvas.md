# DP5: Flag-to-Fan Conversion Insights: product canvas

> Synthetic project. All roles are fictional. The headings are the same for every product.

## Customer
Fan marketing and club marketing (aggregates only) and the privacy lead (as approver).

## Problem
Nobody can say whether flag turns families into fans, or how many families a club may contact, without profiling children or breaking consent.

## Value proposition
**Driver for G3 (directly moves the goal).**

## Consumption type
Internal. Club-facing aggregates only.

## Product type
Insights (restricted).

## Owner role and supporting roles
Data Product Manager, NFL FLAG Insights (fictional). Supporting: privacy and compliance (approver), analytics, fan marketing.

## Key dependencies
DP2 (households), the fan engagement source, consent capture in DP1, the consent provenance and activation funnel (ADR-019), the household linkage governance (ADR-009), the age-tier rules (ADR-022).

## What we will NOT do
No child-level targeting. No marketing to any minor. No fuzzy household linkage. No carry-over of a youth profile to the adult. No conclusion about whether a club is a third party (an open question for counsel). Analytics is not activation.

## Expected shelf life
Short-to-medium; reviewed each season and on any change in the consent rules.
