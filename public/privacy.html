Do we know what families agreed to?

What this means. A family can only be contacted if we know what they agreed to. Records marked partly or not recorded cannot be used.

What this means. This is why a consent email is not a marketing opt-in. We need the wording, the date and the purpose.

What to do. Ask your league to record the form version and the date with every consent.

What to do. Fix the weakest source first. Check that its form names each purpose.

Be careful. This says what is on record. It does not say a family would agree today.

Which purposes does each consent form cover?

What this means. Only a form that names club marketing can carry a club-marketing opt-in.

What this means. A yes here is needed but not enough. Some purposes also need their own consent.

What to do. Do not market to families who signed a form that does not name it.

What to do. Update the older forms and keep a record of the version each family signed.

Be careful. This is not legal advice. Whether a form is enough is a question for counsel.

What this means. Rebuilding from anonymized facts keeps every figure. Re-running matching on the anonymized data moves most figures.

What to do. No action. Keep the anonymized counts.

How identifying is the analyst share?

What this means. Clubs do not receive the analyst share. This chart is for the privacy lead.

What this means. A unique record can be picked out with the four fields birth year, ZIP3, sex and country. That is why the share is pseudonymized and its use needs a stated purpose.

What to do. No action.

What to do. Keep the analyst share inside the team and record the purpose of each use.

Be careful. Pseudonymized is not anonymous.
