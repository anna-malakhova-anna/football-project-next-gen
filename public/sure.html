1 · Range

Why is there a range, not one number?

The count is about 7,430. Missing leagues could add up to about 1,560 more.

Why is there a range, not one number?

What this means. The tick is the count. Each bar is one reason the true number could differ. We do not add the three bars together.

What this means. The top bar is about matching people. Two children wrongly treated as one would sit in that bar.

What to do. Ask the leagues that sent no file to send one. That narrows the widest bar.

What to do. Check the matching bar each year for signs that siblings were merged.

Be careful. The bar for leagues that sent no file is an estimate, not a hard limit.

For analysts

Three intervals from mart_uncertainty_decomposition, per year, never added (ADR-015): identity (what the resolver could move the count by), data-quality exclusions (strict versus lenient counting rules), and coverage (organizations that sent no file; an estimate, not a bound). Values are rounded to the nearest 10 players.

2 · Girls' trend

Is the girls' trend real?

Girls are 47% of players. Missing gender moves that by less than 1 point.

Is the girls' trend real?

What this means. The dot is the girls' share among players with a recorded gender. The grey bar shows how far it could move if every blank were a girl, or a boy.

What this means. Gender is left blank most often for the youngest players and in spreadsheets. The bar shows what those blanks cost.

What to do. Collect gender on every form for the youngest age groups first.

What to do. Keep gender optional where families object. A wider bar is the price.

Be careful. Age groups with fewer than 10 players are left out. Nothing is guessed.

For analysts

Girls' share of youth by division (mart_missingness_bias, KPI girls_share_of_youth), national. The bounds assign every unknown gender to girls, then to boys (Manski bounds); the width is reported in share points. Nothing is imputed.

3 · Methods

Do the methods work?

In 12 of 15 checks, the true number fell inside our range.Synthetic evaluation — not observable in production

Do the methods work?

What this means. The cross is the true number from the made-up data. A green bar contains it. An orange bar misses it.

What this means. The matching bar contains the truth every year. That is evidence siblings are not hiding in the count.

What to do. Treat the range for leagues that sent no file as a guide, not a promise.

What to do. No action. Re-run this check whenever the matching rules change.

Be careful. This only works on made-up data, where the true answer is known. Real data has no answer key.

Each component's interval against the generator's answer key (eval_uncertainty_decomposition), evaluation only. The coverage estimate was not tuned after the answer key was read, and its interval misses the truth in some years; that is reported, not hidden.

4 · Fix early

What is the payoff of fixing problems early?

Fixing a league's top problems lifts its grades. It does not find missing leagues.Synthetic evaluation — not observable in production

What is the payoff of fixing problems early?

What this means. Grey dots are grades before. Green diamonds are after. Half of the struggling leagues acted on their fix list in the last season.

What this means. Better forms and fewer blanks lift quality without collecting more personal data.

What to do. Send fix lists to the leagues that struggle most.

What to do. Prefer form changes to extra data collection.

Be careful. This is a simulated scenario on made-up data. It shows what could happen, not what will.
