Appendix: school and recreation programs

A stretch source: how many children do schools and recreation departments report?

A stretch source: school districts and recreation departments report how many children are in their programs. The counts have no people in them, so they are never added to participants.

Combined reach: participants plus school and recreation program headcounts

What this shows. The reported headcount from schools and recreation departments (line) and the range for participants plus that headcount (band): the low end assumes as much overlap as is possible, the high end none.

What it does not show. A single combined number: aggregates cannot be matched to people, so some children are counted twice, and a PE unit is exposure, not participation. Programs are small cells and are suppressed where few.
