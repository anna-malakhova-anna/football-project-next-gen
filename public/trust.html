Sibling and twin false merges by thresholdSynthetic evaluation — not observable in production

What this shows. In plain terms: merging siblings is not just a counting error; it mixes two children's personal data, which is a privacy incident. The chart counts how many times two different children of one household were merged at each threshold.

Design choice. The final matcher has a household guard against sibling merges, and a data steward reviews the borderline "probable" pairs.

What it does not show. That a merge is harmless, or real-world performance: the sibling and twin merges that remain are the incidents that matter.
