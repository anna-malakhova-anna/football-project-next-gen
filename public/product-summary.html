3 · Who are the products for?

Six personas, and what we'd ask them.

There are no real users yet, so these are assumptions to test.

International growth lead

Serves G1

Decide which countries to invest in

We would ask: Which decisions did you make last year without data?

NFL FLAG domestic operations lead

Serves G2

Know where flag is growing and where leagues are struggling

We would ask: Which numbers do you distrust today, and why?

Club youth and community manager

Serves G2, G3

Grow flag in our market and connect families to the club

We would ask: What would you do differently with a market view?

Fan and club marketing analyst

Serves G3

Show that flag creates fans, and reach families who agreed to be reached

We would ask: What evidence would convince finance that flag drives fandom?

Privacy and compliance lead

Serves G3 (guardrail)

Make sure children's data is never misused

We would ask: What would make you comfortable approving club access?

League operator (volunteer)

Serves G2 (supplier)

Run the league and send the data that is asked for

We would ask: What is the most you would fill in at registration?
