# Terms of use: DP6 Global Markets Outlook

> Synthetic project; every role is fictional. Plain-language terms, not legal terms. Project policy informed by public rules, not legal advice. DRAFT until Anna approves.

## Permitted uses
- Seeing which program markets have participation data good enough to read, and which do not.
- Reading a market's direction only when it clears the noise floor.
- Deciding which markets to onboard next.

## Prohibited uses
- Quoting a planning scenario as a count of players, or placing it beside measured values.
- Treating a market with no data as having no players.
- Turning club proxy returns (events, games) into a player count.

## Who can request access, and how
Executives and the international growth lead by default. Others request through the feedback issue template (label dp6).

## Retention
Aggregates are kept with the season they describe. The legal regime per market is an unverified assumption until the privacy lead checks it.

## What happens when a published number is restated
A change to the status, direction or scenario rules is a new ADR that names the evaluation it saw (ADR-025), and bumps this product's version.

## How to report misuse
Open an issue with the label dp6.
