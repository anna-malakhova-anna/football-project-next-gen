# Terms of use: DP4 US Flag Growth & Club Market Insights

> Synthetic project; every role is fictional. Plain-language terms, not legal terms. Project policy informed by public rules, not legal advice.

## Permitted uses
- Seeing participation and data gaps in a club market.
- Telling growth from better reporting by reading the range before the line.
- Asking leagues that sent no data to send it.

## Prohibited uses
- Individual targeting of a family or a child.
- Re-identification, including by differencing two published numbers.
- Child-level marketing.
- Presenting a market as a club's real territory: markets are a project approximation (ADR-016).

## Who can request access, and how
Domestic operations and club youth and community managers receive the club share. A club asks through the feedback issue template (label dp4); the privacy lead approves access to a new club.

## Retention
Aggregates only; kept with their methodology version.

## What happens when a published number is restated
A change to the market mapping or the counting rules restates the affected years and bumps the major version. The change is listed in products/CHANGELOG.md.

## How to report misuse
Open an issue with the label dp4, or tell the privacy lead directly if a number was used to target a family.
