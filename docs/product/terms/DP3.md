# Terms of use: DP3 Global Participation Insights

> Synthetic project; every role is fictional. Plain-language terms, not legal terms. Project policy informed by public rules, not legal advice.

## Permitted uses
- Comparing countries, always with the range shown.
- Reading the global count as 'registered', not 'played'.
- Deciding where to invest in federation relationships.

## Prohibited uses
- Quoting a count without its range.
- Publishing a country count under the small-cell size.
- Treating a country that sent no data as having no players.

## Who can request access, and how
Executives and the international growth lead by default. Others request through the feedback issue template (label dp3).

## Retention
Aggregates are kept with their methodology version. Snapshots are published and versioned (ADR-007).

## What happens when a published number is restated
A methodology change bumps the major version of this product and restates published numbers (ADR-007, docs/methodology/CHANGELOG.md).

## How to report misuse
Open an issue with the label dp3.
