# Terms of use: DP1 League Registration Intake

> Synthetic project; every role is fictional. Plain-language terms, not legal terms. Project policy informed by public rules, not legal advice.

## Permitted uses
- Onboarding a source through the playbook and watching its quality each season.
- Sending a league its own scorecard and fix list.
- Planning which fields the minimum data standard asks for.

## Prohibited uses
- Re-identifying a child from an intake file.
- Adding a field to the data standard without a named purpose and the privacy lead's review.
- Publishing one league's scorecard outside the data team and that league.

## Who can request access, and how
Data stewards and data engineering have the scorecard and fix list by default. A league operator receives only their own league's. Anyone else asks the data product manager through the feedback issue template (label dp1); the privacy lead approves anything beyond the ops audience.

## Retention
Scorecards and fix lists are kept for the life of the methodology version that produced them. The intake files themselves follow the retention rules in docs/retention.md and ADR-017.

## What happens when a published number is restated
A scorecard restates when the rule registry or the grade bands change (ADR-014). The change is listed in products/CHANGELOG.md and the methodology CHANGELOG, and affected leagues are told.

## How to report misuse
Open an issue with the label dp1 and describe the use. Do not paste any real name, birth date or contact detail.
