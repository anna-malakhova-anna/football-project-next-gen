# ADR-022: Age tiers, guardian consent and the strictest-standard default (Phase 6)

- **Status:** built for the demonstration (2026-10-18). **Every jurisdiction row is `verified = N` until Anna checks it**, and the source URLs are hers to supply. Project policy informed by public rules, **not legal advice**.
- **Synthetic data.** No real child is involved.

## Context
Goal G3 asks whether flag participants and their families become fans, "while protecting children". Phases 3 to 5 applied the protections piecemeal (guardian contacts only, NULL consent is not consent, household not child, age-out deletion). Phase 6 states them once, as data (`age_consent_rules`), and enforces them in one place (`int_consent_permissions`).

## Decision
1. **Four tiers**, from the Phase 1 age convention (completed years on Dec 31 of the season year, ADR-002): **U13**, **13_15**, **16_17**, **ADULT**. A participant whose age cannot be computed is **UNKNOWN** and is evaluated with the strictest minor row, never as an adult. Boundaries are `dbt_project.yml` vars.
2. **Rules are rows of a seed** (`age_consent_rules`, one per jurisdiction and age band; `age_consent_jurisdiction_map` maps a participant's country and region to a jurisdiction; an unlisted country gets `DEFAULT`). The seed carries who consents, allowed and prohibited purposes, a paraphrased basis note, the source URL (blank, Anna's), `verified` and `project_policy`. A generated document, `docs/product/age-consent-rules.md`, lists every row and the open items.
3. **Strictest-standard default.** Where a rule is unknown or unverified, the most protective row applies: **guardian-only for every minor**, profiling and partner disclosure prohibited, and no direct marketing to a minor. Every minor row is therefore `GUARDIAN` today; the legal differences between jurisdictions (for example the California 13 to 15 opt-in, Quebec's under-14 rule, GDPR Article 8's per-country ages) are recorded in the basis notes but do not yet change a decision, because the project policy is stricter than all of them.
4. **Household, not child.** A permission is evaluated for the **guardian** of a household and is never inherited by a child. `int_consent_permissions` is keyed by household and purpose and carries no participant column. **The most restrictive child governs**: the youngest minor in the household decides any household-level activation, because a message such as "your child plays in our league" is about the child.
5. **A blank is never a yes.** Permitted is `Y` only when the rules allow the purpose and it is covered: league operations by the registration itself; every other purpose by a guardian consent whose provenance is fully known (what, when, which purpose). NOT_CAPTURED, DECLINED, REVOKED and incomplete provenance all give `N` (the Phase 1 to 3 NULL rule).
6. **Analytics is not activation.** In `mart_conversion_funnel_by_tier`, registered, linked, engaged and purchased are analytics only (permitted under the household linkage policy, ADR-009); only the last step, consented to club marketing, uses consent. The funnel is counts only, split by the youngest child's tier, and hides a step when it or the step it follows is below the small-cell size.
7. **Fresh consent at 18, no carry-over.** An adult becomes their own fan record only with fresh consent. The youth-to-adult fan transition is therefore **not measurable by design** (catalog metric `youth_to_adult_fan_transition`). The only route to an aggregate figure is a **proposed** self-reported "played as a youth?" answer at adult registration (`docs/data-standard.md`, proposed additions): optional, aggregate only, not in the standard until accepted.

## What this does not decide
- **Whether a club is a "third party"** under the amended COPPA Rule is an open question for counsel (CLAUDE.md, ADR-019, the questions file). The COPPA row records the rule as a paraphrase marked unverified; no model or page concludes anything about clubs.
- No row has been legally reviewed. Legal text is paraphrased; nothing here is a finding.

## Findings from building and testing it
- **Tiers (participant-years, all seasons):** U13 11,920; 13_15 3,910; 16_17 3,095; ADULT 2,171; UNKNOWN 247. Jurisdictions: US_FED 11,848, US_CA 1,955, CA_OTHER 3,186, CA_QC 757, EU 1,125, GB 846, DEFAULT 1,626 (Mexico, Japan). Phase 7 renamed Panama to Spain, which moved its 291 participant-years from DEFAULT to EU (before: EU 834, DEFAULT 1,917); no permission changed, because both rule sets make every Spanish household guardian-only with nothing captured ([ADR-024](ADR-024-global-reference-data-and-spain-rename.md)).
- **Activatable households** (guardian CLUB_MARKETING permitted Y, by the youngest child's tier): U13 525 of 5,793 (9.1%); 13_15 190 of 2,226 (8.5%); 16_17 187 of 2,246 (8.3%); ADULT 65 of 1,350 (4.8%); UNKNOWN age hidden (under 10).
- **Tests added:** `consent_permissions_never_grant_from_a_blank`, `activation_counts_only_guardian_consented_households`, `a_participant_turning_18_carries_no_fan_link` (**vacuous in this data**: the generator ends a child's seasons at the youth age maximum, so no participant plays on as an adult; it is written to bite when such data exists), the `verified` checks on the seed, and `tests/test_phase6_age_rules.py` (band coverage, minor rows guardian-only, the generated doc in sync, and the contact-field scan).
- **The contact-field scan** (`tests/test_phase6_age_rules.py`) now covers **every schema** and passes only because the following holds. Restricted zones (may hold identifying data): the pipeline (`raw`, `staging`, `intermediate`), identity (`identity`, `marts`), linkage (`linkage`, `pii_store`), the Phase 5 and 6 working zones (`phase5_restricted`, `phase6_restricted`, `quality_restricted`) and the quarantined answer key (`eval`). Everything else must hold no contact, name or exact birth-date value column, except three shares that carry identifiers on purpose by audience policy: `share_marketing__contact_list` (guardian emails, with `contact_role`, no child key), `share_media__roster` (names of participants whose photo consent is on record; no contact detail or birth date) and `share_ops` (the ops audience).
- **Moved into restricted zones (2026-10-18):** `stg_p5_registrations` and `p5_idr_input_records` to `phase5_restricted`; `dq_standardized_records` to the new `quality_restricted`; the adult fan account dimension `dim_fan_account` to `linkage` (it holds the account holder's email and phone). Stale tables left in the local warehouse by retired models (`phase5.p5_standardized_records`, `p5_standard_conformance`, `p5_standard_summary`, `p5_child`, `p5_clusters_probable`, `p5_market_age_cells`, `dq_fix_list`, and `phase5_restricted.p5_child_market_weight`) and the old copies were dropped; a fresh build never creates them.
- **The turning-18 test is no longer vacuous.** The check is a model (`int_age_out_carryover_check`) with a dbt unit test (`age_out_carryover_is_caught`) that gives it five hand-built participants and requires exactly one row back: P1, who ages out (13 to 15 in 2022, adult in 2024) of a household with children (H1) that has a fan link. The others must NOT be returned: P2 (same, but an adult-only household), P3 (stays a minor), P4 (never a minor), P5 (ages out, no fan link). The data test requires the model to be empty on real data.
  - **Without the rule** (the line `where h.is_adult_household is false` replaced by `where false`, so the check can return nothing) the unit test **fails on the aged-out fixture specifically**: `FAIL 1 int_age_out_carryover_check::age_out_carryover_is_caught`, with the diff `--- participant_id P1, household_id H1, first_minor_year 2022, first_adult_year 2024` (the expected row is missing from the actual output). This was run twice: once in the real project and reverted, and again on 2026-10-18 on an isolated copy of the project and warehouse, so that a running build was not disturbed.
  - **With the rule** (the committed model) the same unit test **passes**: `PASS int_age_out_carryover_check::age_out_carryover_is_caught`, `PASS=1 WARN=0 ERROR=0`. The repository model was never left broken: its working-tree hash equals its staged hash (`git hash-object` = `418c4294…`) and `git diff` against the staged copy is empty.

## Consequences
- The conversion funnel by tier can be shown on the Data product tab as aggregate, suppressed counts, with the unverified jurisdictions listed openly.
- Changing any rule is a one-row seed change plus a generated document, and a stricter-than-the-law row stays stricter until a verified row replaces it.
