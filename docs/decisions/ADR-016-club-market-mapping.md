# ADR-016: The club-market lens: an approximation, allocation, and no invented denominators

- **Status:** Accepted for step 9 of `phase-5-spec.md` (2026-10-14). Numbered 016 (the spec calls it ADR-014; see [ADR-013](ADR-013-phase5-reconciliation.md)).
- **Synthetic data.** The markets, their names and every count are generated. **The mapping is a project-defined approximation, not any club's actual territory definition.**

## Context
Clubs ask what they can see and trust in their own market. The program's data has registrations with ZIP codes but no notion of a club market, and club territories are not public in a form this project can use. The spec asks for a domestic lens with coverage, quality grades and an activation funnel per market.

## Decisions
1. **A project approximation of 32 club markets** (`club_market_map`): the project's ZIP3 universe, grouped into contiguous ZIP3 blocks inside each state (the number per state is configuration, `phase5.club_markets`), plus `NO_CLUB_MARKET` for ZIP3s that belong to no club. Every row says so (`approximation_note`). A market is named from its id ("TX club market 01"), never after a real club, team or metro. Whether clubs' real territory definitions differ is a stakeholder question (added to the list).
2. **Shared markets are split by weight, equal by default.** An NY/NJ-style or Los Angeles-style metro is modelled by giving a ZIP3 two markets with weights 0.5 and 0.5 (`mapping_method = SHARED_EQUAL_SPLIT`). The weights of a ZIP3 sum to 1, so allocated participants add back up to the total; the equal split is a default and labelled as one. A real allocation (by club affiliation or population) can replace the weights without changing any model.
3. **Allocation is made once** (`int_participant_year_market`): each participant-year, with weights summing to 1, by home ZIP3; Canadian and international participants are `OUTSIDE_US` (the lens is domestic by design, said on the page). A US participant with no usable ZIP is **allocated** by where the *placed* participants of the same organization and year live (else the organization's state), and reported **separately** from the placed count (`participants_zip_placed` and `participants_unplaced_allocated`): an estimate that is never silently merged into a market's figure. One that cannot be allocated stays `UNALLOCATED`. Every market-level model reads this one table.
4. **No invented denominators.** Participation rates need a population denominator, and Phase 3 forbids inventing figures. `population_denominators` ships empty; `scripts/fetch_acs_youth_population.py` is for Anna to run herself (US Census ACS table B01001, ZCTA aggregated to ZIP3, source, URL and date on every row; **not tested against the live API from the build environment**). Until it is loaded every market row reads `denominator not loaded` and the rate is NULL; once loaded the rate carries the label "illustrative only: synthetic participant counts over real population denominators". The synthetic stub of Phase 5 v1 was retired.
5. **Coverage per market** (`mart_market_coverage`): known versus reporting organizations (the organization registry, spread over markets by each organization's ZIP3), the dark organizations' estimated participants (`int_dark_org_coverage`, the same estimate as the national decomposition, now factored out and checked to give identical national numbers), the share of records fit for GEO, and the purpose grades of `dq_scorecard_market`.
6. **The same marts, sliced by market**: `mart_activation_funnel` (weighted household counts; per-market households add back up to the national figure), `mart_missingness_bias` (girls' share of youth per market with bounds, and `club_market_attribution`: the share of US participants in the market, where participants with no usable ZIP are the unknowns, bounded by "all of them elsewhere" and "all of them here").

## Known limits
- Allocated counts are fractional by design; a market's figure is not a head count of named people.
- The equal split of a shared ZIP3 is arbitrary. A club that shares a metro sees the *whole* market in its row, not half: sharing clubs read the same market-level figures.
- A market with no reporting organization has no organization-based coverage estimate; the registry places an organization by its modal ZIP3, so an organization near a block boundary can sit in a neighbouring market.
