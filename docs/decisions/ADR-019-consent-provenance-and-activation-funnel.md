# ADR-019: Consent provenance and the activation funnel

- **Status:** Accepted for step 8 of `phase-5-spec.md` (2026-10-14). Not one of the six ADRs the spec names; it records decisions the spec leaves open.
- **Synthetic data.** Wording versions, purposes and every count are generated. Whether a real form's wording covers a purpose is an open question for the privacy lead (see `docs/questions-for-stakeholders.md`); nothing here is a legal finding.

## Context
Phase 4 captured consent by channel for fan accounts. Registrations carry consent too (US photo and marketing boxes, Canada's `consents` object, an international media box), and in Phase 5 each organization's form uses one of four wording versions, two of which name no club-marketing purpose. The question: for which households do we *know* what the parent agreed to, when and on which wording, and for how many may a club make contact?

## Decisions
1. **Events, keyed by household.** `int_consent_event` (restricted schema `linkage`) has one row per consent event of the modelled sources: `FORM_ACCEPTANCE` (the form's bundled purposes, LEAGUE_OPS and LEAGUE_COMMS where the wording names them; with an unknown wording the status is UNKNOWN, because *what* was accepted is unknown), `PHOTO_OPT`, `MARKETING_OPT_IN` and `REVOCATION`. A form acceptance **never** grants CLUB_MARKETING or PHOTO_MEDIA: those have their own boxes. A blank box is no event (not captured is not FALSE). A test checks all three.
2. **Provenance is three questions**: which wording (the consent text version, or UNKNOWN), when (`captured_at`), and does the wording name this event's purpose. `KNOWN_FULL` = all three, `KNOWN_PARTIAL` = at least one, `UNKNOWN` = none. Method is captured by no source: UNKNOWN. A revocation is `KNOWN_PARTIAL` and is not evidence of consent.
3. **An assumption, configurable**: the registration time is the consent time (`phase5.dq.consent_time_from_registration`, default true), because a form carries its consent in the same submission; Canada's own `consent_captured_at` is its registration time. With it false only an explicit timestamp counts and, since no US source has one, no US household can have full provenance.
4. **Household status is the latest relevant event as of Dec 31 of each year** (`int_household_year_consent`): a revocation is an event and a later one wins; on a tie in time a denial wins, then incomplete evidence over full, the conservative reading. `GRANTED_FULL` is the only status that counts as consenting; `GRANTED_INCOMPLETE` (an opt-in on a form that never named the purpose) is **not** evidence of that consent.
5. **The funnel separates the two kinds of loss** (`mart_activation_funnel`, per year and attributed market, national row `ALL`): registrations, participants, households, households with a valid contact, households with known provenance, households consenting. Steps 2 and 3 are `UNIT` (a person registers more than once a year; siblings share a household), steps 4 and 5 are `DQ`, and step 6 is both: declined and revoked households are `PRIVACY`, opt-ins on forms that never named the purpose and choices never captured are `DQ`. `lost_dq` and `lost_privacy` give the exact split and `mart_activation_funnel_loss` the reasons. The chart colours the two differently: it is the bridge between the data steward and the privacy lead.
6. **Counts only; the child is never joined.** The funnel and loss tables carry no participant, household, registration or name column, and the household-level models carry no participant column. A test checks the columns, that the funnel is nested, that the loss splits add up and that the national consenting count equals an independent re-derivation.
7. **Reconciliation with v1.** The Phase 5 v1 consent chain for the six channel feeds (`p5_consent_event`, `p5_household_*`, `p5_consent_funnel`) stays for those Contracted feeds, which are outside the counts, until the retention job moves to the identity and linkage zones (spec step 11). Its purpose names are the old ones (`NFL_CLUB_MARKETING`); `consent_purposes` has the spec's.

## Findings (synthetic)
- **72% of recorded marketing opt-ins are not fully evidenced.** Of 9,097 opt-ins, 2,525 are on a form whose wording names the purpose; 6,572 are not (they sit on the wording versions without CLUB_MARKETING). They count as `GRANTED_INCOMPLETE`.
- **Nationally in 2025**, of 6,986 households 474 are consenting with full provenance. The loss to consent is 3,518 declined and 37 revoked (privacy) against 1,514 data-quality (1,476 opt-ins on forms that never named the purpose).
- **The Phase 3 marketing contact list does not read the wording or a revocation.** Of its 3,087 contacts, 430 match a household that is `GRANTED_FULL`, 1,373 an opt-in on a form without the purpose, and 10 a revoked household. This is reported, not changed: whether to tighten a Phase 3 share is a decision for the privacy lead (question added to the stakeholder list).
